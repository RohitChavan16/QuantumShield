## 1. Narrative Arc

*Setup → Attack → Detection → Explanation → Business Impact → Differentiator.*

1. **Setup (10s):** "Meet a bank treasury employee's laptop and their bank account — completely normal, until now."
2. **Attack (20s):** Trigger the "Treasury Compromise" scenario — a malicious process spawns on the endpoint (visible in a live log tail or terminal window for authenticity).
3. **Detection (30s):** Within seconds, a transaction fires from that same endpoint. The Fusion Dashboard lights up with a CRITICAL alert — live timer overlay shows the sub-1.5s latency.
4. **Explanation (40s):** Click into the alert — SHAP-style bar chart, Gemini narrative, ask the AI panel one live follow-up question.
5. **Business impact (30s):** Cut to Executive Dashboard — false-positive-reduction chart (before/after correlation), ₹ fraud prevented.
6. **Differentiator (30s):** Cut to Quantum Dashboard — "and separately, we're the only team addressing the *quantum* half of this problem statement" — click "Simulate ML-KEM Migration," watch the number move.
7. **Close (10s):** Restate the one-liner: *"Your SIEM sees the malware. Your fraud engine sees the transfer. We're the only ones who see it's the same attack."*
